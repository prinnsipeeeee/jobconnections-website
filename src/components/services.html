SIMPLE PROCESS

How to Avail Our Services

Foreign employers shall submit the following requirements for accreditation with the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration (POEA).

Required Documents

  • ✔ Special Power of Attorney
  • ✔ Recruitment Agreement
  • ✔ Authority to Recruit Foreign Labor
  • ✔ Employment Contract
  • ✔ Company License
  • ✔ Manpower Request

Important Notice

All documents must be verified and sealed by the Philippine Overseas Labor Office (POLO) and the Philippine Embassy before processing.
